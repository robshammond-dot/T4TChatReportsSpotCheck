Spot Checking Chats

How to find a chat that stands out in the Fresh reports, open it, and see what actually happened.

A

Why spot check

Agent assessment is not part of our current Fresh plan. Spot checks do the same job: the reports show where to look, and the chat shows what actually happened.

  • Numbers point, chats explain. A slow response time tells you something happened. The chat tells you what.
  • Pick chats that stand out, not chats at random. The steps below show how to find them.
  • One chat is one moment. Look at a few before drawing a conclusion about a consultant.
B

Find the chat that stands out

Every chat behind a report is listed in its underlying data. You can only see it once the chart or table is expanded.

  1. Open the report, for example the New Label Report or the AI Agent Performance Report 2026.
  2. Hover over the chart or table you want to look into, and click the Expand icon (two small arrows) at its top right.
  3. In the expanded view, scroll down and click Show underlying data.
  4. Look down the columns for a figure that stands out from the rest.
What stands out. Our first reply target is 10 minutes. In the example, most first responses are a few minutes, but one is 52m 40s and another is 2h 44m 51s. Those are the chats to open.
Many pages. The underlying data runs over many pages. Use the arrows in a column heading to sort it, so the largest figures come to the top, or use Next to move through the pages.
C

Watch out for look-alike columns. Some reports also have a column called Conversation ID that holds letters, numbers and dashes, for example 663a9953-9513-46f6-.... That is not the one to copy. The same goes for Customer ID, account_id and Interaction. Use one of the two columns in the table above.
D

Open a chat from a Conversation number

For the 16-digit number from the Conversation column. There is no button in the report that opens the chat, so we use the address bar instead.

  1. Copy the number from the Conversation column, for example 1186361753115529.
  2. Open any chat in Fresh.
  3. Look at the address bar. The address ends in /conversation/ followed by a long number.
  4. Select only that last number, paste the Conversation number in its place, and press Enter. The chat you copied opens.
Change only the last number. Leave the rest of the address exactly as it is.
E

Open a chat from an AI Agent Ticket ID

For the 8-digit number from the AI Agent Ticket ID column. These chats are found in Tommy's own section of Fresh, not in the reports.

Open Tommy's conversation logs

  1. Copy the number from the AI Agent Ticket ID column, for example 29674159.
  2. Open Tommy's conversation logs with the button above. In Fresh this is AI Agent Studio, Tommy, Analyze, Conversation logs.
  3. Paste the number into the search box. The box says Search by Conversation ID, but this is where the AI Agent Ticket ID goes.
  4. The chat appears in the list, with its status, for example Handed off.
This number does not work in the address bar. An AI Agent Ticket ID only finds the chat in Tommy's conversation logs. The address bar method in Part D is only for the 16-digit Conversation number.
F

Reading the chat

Go back to the figure that stood out, and look for what explains it.

  • The messages show who replied and at what time, so you can see where the time went.
  • In the Fresh inbox, the grey notes between messages show how the chat reached the consultant, for example:
Conversation was assigned to group Consulting Team by Topic group mapping "SLA Policy" SLA policy applied Conversation was assigned to Agent A by IntelliAssign

A chat can also be assigned by a person, in which case the note shows their name instead of IntelliAssign.

Check the label

Every spot check is also a label check. Labels are often chosen incorrectly or left off, and many reports depend on them, so adding and correcting them keeps the numbers accurate.

  • Is a label missing? Under Resolved by, a chat with no label shows Add Label. Chats Tommy closed automatically have no label, so they stand out. Click Add Label and choose the right one.
  • Does the label match the chat? Compare the label with what actually happened, for example a chat labelled Information only where the customer asked for a quote. To change it, hover next to the label and click the edit icon (the pencil), then choose the right one.
  • Booking Converted especially. We use this label for now to track chats that turned into bookings, so it only counts if it is used correctly.
Before raising it with a consultant. Look at a few of their chats, and read them alongside their numbers in Chat Reports Made Simple, Part C: Reading team numbers together.
